# Frostline FL-18i inverter split AC: user manual (synthetic)

Fictional manual written for HomeKeep. "Frostline" is not a real brand.

## 2.1 Remote control batteries
The remote uses two AAA batteries. A blank display means the batteries need replacing.

## 2.3 Power supply
The indoor unit needs its wall switch in the ON position.

## 3.2 Operating modes
The FL-18i cools only in COOL mode, shown by the snowflake symbol on the remote display.

## 3.3 Fan speed
Fan speed cycles through Low, Medium, High and Auto with the FAN button.

## 3.4 Temperature setting
Set a temperature below the room temperature; 24 degrees is recommended. Allow 10 minutes for the room to respond.

## 5.1 Getting the best cooling
Open doors, windows and direct sunlight reduce cooling noticeably.

## 5.2 Keeping air paths clear
Keep at least 30 cm clear in front of the indoor unit so air can flow freely.

## 6.4 Water dripping indoors
Water dripping from the indoor unit usually needs a service visit to clear the drain.

## 7 Display codes
- E1: room temperature sensor fault. Restart once with the remote; if E1 returns, call service.
- E3: outdoor unit protection. Do not restart repeatedly; arrange service.

Servicing inside the unit, including the filter housing, refrigerant and wiring, is for trained technicians only.
